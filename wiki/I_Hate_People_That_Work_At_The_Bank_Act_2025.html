MediaWiki 1.24 and newer no longer automatically enables installed skins (see Manual: Skin autodiscovery). You can paste the following lines into LocalSettings.php to enable all installed skins:

wfLoadSkin( 'MonoBook' );
wfLoadSkin( 'Timeless' );
wfLoadSkin( 'Vector' );

I Hate People That Work At The Bank Act 2025

I Hate People That Work At The Bank! Shhh I Work At The Bank. Here Is A Dollar Amendment Act 2025

Passed by

Authored by Thyme (thymaerys), Speaker of the 147th Senate

Preamble

Whereas casinos are obviously financial institutions and should be defined as such,

Article I

§1. Article 1 §3 of the Bank Note Regulations Act 2025 shall be amended and shall state the following: 

 §3. A “financial institution” shall be defined as any company or corporation that provides services relating to the management of currency, its growth, its expenditure, its security, its exchange, or preventing the loss of currency. Casinos shall also be defined as financial institutions and may be referenced as such in other pieces of legislation.

§2. Upon passage of this bill, all casinos shall be made to reapply for their business license with the Department of the Treasury as to ensure they are following all guidelines set out for established financial institutions.
