Rejuvenation Act 2025

Passed by the Senate and signed by the President on 30 August 2025.

Written by egg

Preamble

Whereas, chat dies way to much;

Whereas, let's bring it back to life;

Article 1: Establishment

§1. A role shall be created called “dead chat pings”.

§2. This role shall be able to be pinged by all members of SimDem.

§3. This role shall be obtained within the Channels and Roles section of the Discord.

Article 2: Implementation

§1. This bill shall go into effect after passage in the Senate and signature from the President.

Explanatory Notes

This bill allows all members of SimDemocracy the ability to ping this role once it is created. Pinging this role would ping people who selected to get dead chat pings. They would have selected it via the Channels & Roles section, which is at the top of channels on mobile. This role would have the default Gray coloration just as the other member ping roles, and members would be free to remove this anytime.
