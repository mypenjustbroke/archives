If you have just installed MediaWiki
You probably installed from git, or directly from the source code using some other method. This is expected. Try installing some skins from mediawiki.org's skin directory, by:
  • Downloading the tarball installer, which comes with several skins and extensions. You can copy and paste the skins/ directory from it.
  • Downloading individual skin tarballs from mediawiki.org.
  • Using Git to download skins.
Doing this should not interfere with your git repository if you're a MediaWiki developer.
If you have just upgraded MediaWiki
MediaWiki 1.24 and newer no longer automatically enables installed skins (see Manual: Skin autodiscovery). You can paste the following lines into LocalSettings.php to enable all installed skins:
wfLoadSkin( 'MinervaNeue' );
wfLoadSkin( 'MonoBook' );
wfLoadSkin( 'Timeless' );
wfLoadSkin( 'Vector' );
If you have just modified LocalSettings.php
Double-check the skin names for typos.

The T(t)au Specification Act

Author: Matt Cheney

Sponsored by: Speaker Matt Cheney

Preamble

Whereas Tau is a pillar of the community and deserves the respect of proper capitalization of their name and currency named for them;

Whereas showing respect to others will encourage more people to do things worthy of respect;

Whereas all citizens should know the difference;

Article 1: Specifications

§1. The short form of the user @bternarytau’s name shall be formally recognized as Tau, specifically with the capital “T”

§2. The currency of SimDemocracy shall be formally recognized as tau, specifically with the lowercase “t”.

Article 2: Penalties

§1. If any official government document or communication refers to “Tau” as “tau”, Tau has the legal authority to yell or crash out at the author or poster of said document or communication.

§2. If a private citizen makes the same mistake there are no penalties, but they should be ashamed of themselves.

Article 3: Implementation

§1. This Act shall come into effect as soon as it passes the Senate.
