Preamble

Whereas consolidating similar acts reduces the amount of stuff in the archives;

Whereas acts with proper formatting are easier to implement;

Whereas there is a need for a structured and transparent system for services, goods, and business advertisements within SimDemocracy;

Whereas there is a need for a structured and transparent system for job postings and advertisements within SimDemocracy;

Whereas a centralized platform for economic activity will enhance accessibility, efficiency, and fair trade among citizens;

Whereas clear regulations and oversight are necessary to ensure proper functionality and fairness of the marketplace;

THE SENATE OF SIMDEMOCRACY PROCLAIMS

Consolidated Common Marketplace Act

Consolidates the Common and Job Marketplace Act and clarifies the consolidated common marketplace act.

Article 1: Replacements

§1 The Common Marketplace Act shall be repealed as it is being consolidated into this act.

§2 The Jobs Marketplace Act shall be repealed as it is being consolidated into this act.

§3 The Consolidated Common Marketplace Act shall be repealed as its being replaced with this amended act.

Article 2: Definitions

§1 Jobs Board: A digital platform where SimDem citizens can post and find job opportunities.

§2 Marketplace Moderator: An employee or contractor of the Department of the Treasury responsible for enforcing rules and removing violations in the marketplace channels.

§3 Stuff For Sale: A digital marketplace where citizens and businesses can post goods or services for sale.

§4 Businesses In Operation: A directory where active businesses can advertise their existence and offerings.

§5 Marketplace Moderator: An employee or contractor of the Department of the Treasury responsible for enforcing rules and removing violations in the marketplace channels.

§6 Inactive Business: A business that does not respond to verification inquiries within the designated time frame.

Article 2: Common Marketplaces

§1 The Department of the Treasury shall create a forum channel called “Jobs Board” under the economy category on Discord.

§2 The Department of the Treasury shall create a forum channel called “Stuff For Sale” under the economy category on Discord.

§3 The Department of the Treasury shall create a channel called “Businesses In Operation” under the economy category on Discord.

Article 3: Stuff For Sale Channel

§1 SimDem citizens or businesses shall be able to post their products or services available for sale in the Stuff For Sale channel.

§1.2 Posts shall include it is a product or service, the name of what’s for sale, a reasonable description, cost to purchase, any special conditions (if applicable), and the Name of the seller (business or individual).

§2 The Department of the Treasury shall assign, employ, or contract a moderator to ensure adherence to rules and remove unrelated or violating content.

§2.1 The Stuff For Sale channel shall have a moderator who may remove job posts that are inactive or discontinued and the post shall be removed after sixty days of inactivity.

Article 4: Businesses In Operation Channel

§1 SimDem citizens with a registered business shall be able to post in the Businesses In Operation channel to highlight their business and provide a digital storefront.

§1.2 Business posts shall include Business name, Description of services/products, and Owner(s)

§2 The Department of the Treasury shall assign, employ, or contract a Moderator to ensure adherence to rules and remove unrelated or violating content.

§2.1 Every two (2) weeks, business owners may receive a verification message from the Marketplace Moderator. If no response is given within one (1) week or the business is confirmed as discontinued, its post may be removed.
§2.2 The Businesses In Operation channel shall have a moderator who will remove job posts that are inactive or discontinued after sixty days of inactivity regardless of the verification process.

Article 5: Jobs Board Channel

Repealed<ref name="luka_amendments">Consolidated Marketplace Amendment</ref>

§1 SimDem citizens or businesses shall be able to post a job offering in the Jobs Board channel.

§1 SimDem citizens or businesses shall be able to post a job offering in the Jobs Board channel for a fee of 50 tau for one week of posting.

§1.1 Job postings shall include the job title, a reasonable description of job responsibilities, and the pay

§2 The Department of the Treasury shall assign, employ, or contract a moderator for the channel to ensure adherence to rules and remove unrelated or violating content.

§2.2 The Jobs Board channel moderator may remove job posts that are inactive or discontinued and shall remove posts after sixty (60) days of inactivity.

§3. All government job offerings shall be advertised on the jobs board channel.<ref name="MTGUTJBA">Amended by the Make the Government Use the Job Board Act</ref>

Article 6: Implementation

§1 Upon passage, this act comes into effect immediately.
