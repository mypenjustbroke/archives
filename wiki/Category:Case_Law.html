Copyright notice

The Judges and Justices hold all rights to the respective verdicts

The judiciary also kept their own records in the Case Law Index, until it reached the file word limit (yes, Google Docs does indeed have a limit on file size). It was created by Danyo, SC and later primarily maintained by Ivy Cactus, SC. A newer version focusing on the decisions of appellate has since been created by Alexa.

You may also find it useful to check Category:Laws Concerning the Judiciary for relevant legislation passed by the Senate.

Consider taking a look at the database compiled by Danyo, that includes laws from other polsims, as well.

Note: that the boxes at the beginning of each case are not binding, but are merely summaries provided by the judge, CLI editor, and/or the Archives Commission.
