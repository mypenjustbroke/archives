6th of November Accord

Passed by the SimDem Senate and signed by the President on 11 November 2025.

Passed by the VoD House, Senate, and signed by the President on 8 November 2025.

Between the Governments of SimDemocracy and Voices of Democracy

CONSIDERING

The shared history between the nations of Voices of Democracy and SimDemocracy, which has fostered mutual respect and understanding;

The differences between our nations that enrich our perspectives and strengthen our collective spirit;

The enduring tradition of cooperation, dialogue, and good will that has historically guided relations between our peoples;

The shared commitment of both nations to democratic values, peaceful coexistence, and constructive engagement;

And recognizing that the issue giving rise to this Accord stems from a difference in perspective, yet remains legitimate and worthy of mutual respect;

The Governments of SimDemocracy and Voices of Democracy RESOLVE

Article I — Principles of Friendship and Cooperation

§1. Both parties reaffirm their dedication to maintaining cordial and constructive relations founded on mutual respect, non-interference, and the peaceful resolution of disputes.

Article II — Diplomatic Communication

§1. Both parties agree to maintain open and continuous channels of communication between their respective governments to facilitate dialogue, prevent misunderstandings, and promote transparency in all matters of bilateral concern.

Article III — Security

§1. Both parties reaffirm their commitment to the existing security framework aimed at jointly addressing and countering shared threats.

§2. Recognizing the resurgence of potential hostile activity, both governments agree to a renewed and equal commitment to preventive and responsive measures to safeguard mutual stability and trust.

§3. Such cooperation may include coordinated monitoring, timely intelligence sharing, and rapid consultation in the event of incidents affecting either state.

Article IV — Commitment to Good Faith

§1. Both parties undertake to act in good faith in all interactions, to refrain from inflammatory or hostile conduct, and to pursue all differences through constructive, diplomatic, and respectful means.

Article V — Acts of Peace

§1. Both parties commit to developing and maintaining measures to prevent actions by their members that could endanger relations or stability within each other’s territories.

§2. Accordingly, no citizen or member of either nation shall engage in hostile or disruptive conduct within the territory or official platforms of the other.

§3. Each government shall, however, uphold and respect the principle of freedom of speech within its own jurisdiction, recognizing that expressions or opinions made domestically are independent of the other nation and shall not constitute a breach of this Accord.

Article VI — Commitment to Democracy

§1. Both parties reaffirm their commitment to strengthening democratic processes and institutions within their respective nations.

§2. Each government shall ensure that citizens or representatives of the other nation are not subject to discrimination in any electoral or participatory process where their involvement is permitted under domestic law.

Article VII – Trans-Server-Communication

§1. A shared, official trans-server chat; hereby referred to as the 6th Chat shall be established to allow citizens and officials of both states to communicate openly, reinforcing the communal ties that have historically strengthened both nations.

§2. The chat is intended to foster friendly interaction, cultural exchange, and diplomatic communication, without prejudice to either government’s sovereignty.

§3. Both governments shall take all necessary measures to establish this chat through the InterChat bot complying with all domestic and higher (TOS) regulation.

Article VIII — Ratification and Review

§1. This Accord shall enter into force upon ratification by the respective legislatures of both states.

§2. It shall remain in effect indefinitely unless terminated by dissent of either party.

§3. Both parties may jointly review this Accord at any time to ensure its continued relevance, effectiveness, and alignment with shared security, social, and diplomatic objectives.

§4. Following any review, the Accord may be renewed, amended, or updated by mutual consent, with all modifications recorded in writing and appended hereto.

SIGNED;
