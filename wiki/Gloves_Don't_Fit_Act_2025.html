The Gloves Don't Fit Act 2025

Passed by the Senate on 24 July 2025, vetoed by the President 25 July 2025, veto override passed by the Senate on 25 July 2025.

Proposed by Xi4 & Notcom

Preamble

Whereas, public defenders deserve a competitive salary compared to the SAO

Whereas, success in court should be rewarded in the OPD as well as the SAO

Article I – Amendment to the Office of Public Defenders Act

§1. Office of Public Defenders Act will be amended as follows: 

 Article 4: Compensation
   §1.1. Public defenders shall be entitled to a bonus of twenty five (25) tau if their defendant receives less than the prosecution’s recommended sentence. 
   §1.2. Public defenders shall be entitled to a bonus of one hundred (100) tau if their defendant is found Not Guilty, in addition to the bonus from §1.1.

Motion 1

Passed by the Senate and signed by the President on 25 July 2025.

§1. Motion for an amendment to the Gloves Don't Fit Act to add a 100 tau bonus if the case is dropped.

Motion 2

Passed by the Senate and signed by the President on 27 July 2025.

I, Senator Nf19_1, hereby present a motion to unfriendly amend the new Article 4, §1.1. of the Order of the Public Defenders Act that The Gloves Don't Fit Act, if the Presidential veto will be overturned, introduces through Article 1, §1. to read as follows: 

 §1.1. Public defenders shall be entitled to a pecuniary bonus of fifty (50) tau if the Defendant they represent in Court recives less than the Prosecution's recommended sentence when they are found to be guilty.
