NovaSM v Dick_head68 [2019] Civ 1

Date of judgment 12th July 2019
Judge Judge lolcheapboosts
Grounds Civil harassment (common law), Bail violation (common law)<ref>This case was done at a time when there was no separation of civil and criminal law. Bail violation would not be considered a tort (civil wrong) nowadays, it would be a crime.</ref>
Verdict
  • D held liable for civil harassment
  • D held liable for bail violation
Result D was ordered to be permanently muted on the Discord and subreddit<ref>Muting is now no longer legally permitted in civil cases. A mute was also not legally permitted during the time of the case, as D was a Senator and enjoyed constitutional legal immunity at the time. The judge was impeached for this ruling on grounds of violating the Constitution.</ref>
Applicable persuasive precedent
  • Civil harassment existed under the common law, [2]
  • Definition of civil harassment [2] was rejected by Dodowarrior44 v FruitRollUp [2020] Civ 2
Appeal
  • Case affirmed by the Court of Appeal
    • Dick_head68 (Appellant) v NovaSM (Respondent) [2019] SDCA 1

JUDGMENT by Judge lolcheapboosts<ref>Paragraph numbering has been retrospectively added.</ref>

[1] On multiple counts of harassment and one case of bail violation [the defendant]<ref>The phrase “the defendant” retrospectively substituted with the Discord tag of D which now shows up as “@Deleted User”</ref> is to be permanently muted on both the discord and subreddit

[2] And harassment is to be defined as; the repeated and unwanted bothering, threatening or anything along those lines by one or more users to one or more users even after being asked to stopped.
