Passed by the Senate and signed by the President on 15 August 2025.

Authored by Democracy-foryou

§1. In accordance with the powers enshrined in the Revised Economy Foundation Act the Senate hereby declares that the following salaries shall be given every week:

§2. In the event anyone holds multiple of these positions, they shall only be paid for the highest paid position, with the exception of the judiciary, judges, and justices.

§2.1. For purposes of pay, a member of the judiciary shall only be recognized as being either judge or justice, not both. However, if a member of the judiciary performs the duties of judge and hears cases before the inferior courts of SimDemocracy in addition to their duties as a Justice for at least fifty-one (51) percent of the pay period, as determined by the Supreme Court Supreme Justice, then they shall receive the combined pay of 500 tau. Furthermore, this sub-clause shall not apply retroactively.

§3. The Senate Payments Act shall be repealed.

§4. DO-DoJ-03-25, Department of Justice Pay Grades, Pay Grade 1 shall be altered to remove Attorney General.

<ref name="delulu-amendment-2026">Amendment to Government Salaries Act 2026, passed on February 20 2026 and not vetoed within 72h.</ref> §5. In the event a Senator does not participate in a Senate vote, their salary for that pay period shall be reduced proportionally.

§5.1. Participation shall mean casting any valid vote, including an abstention.
§5.2. The deduction shall be calculated as: (Missed Votes ÷ Total Votes) × Senator salary for that pay period.
§5.3. Votes from which a Senator is formally excused by the Speaker of the Senate shall not count as Missed Votes.
§5.4. Where there are zero (0) votes in a pay period, no deduction shall apply.

Article Two: Implementation

§1. This shall come into effect upon passage.
