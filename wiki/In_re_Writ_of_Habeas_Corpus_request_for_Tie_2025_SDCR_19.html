MAJORITY OPINION by Judge Brandmal

(with Judge ppatpat agreeing)

Introduction

[1] The petitioner is requesting a writ of Habeas Corpus quashing the arrest warrant of Tie due to “lack of Probable Cause and ordering the release and unbanning of President Tie.”

Considerations

[2] Taking reference to Ed (Appellant) v State of SimDemocracy (Respondent) [2025] SDCR 12, the Court of Review finds that the measure requested is only necessary for obviously unlawful warrants, as the legitimacy of the probable cause finding can be contested in pre-trial, which has to begin soon anyways.

[3] The Court sees no reason to act now, when a lawful corrective procedure is imminent anyways. The petitioner has not made out a prima facie case for their detention being so unlawful as to warrant immediate extraordinary emergency orders of this court. Probable cause for the criminal complaint and charges overall ought to be determined at pre-trial.

[4] As such, the petition is dismissed on its merits.

=Verdict

[5] The petition is dismissed.
