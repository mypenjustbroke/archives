In re Candidacy of Alien Users in the 2nd Parliamentary Election [2020] SDSC 19

Date of judgment 22nd October 2020
Justices
  • Chief Justice SeaOtter
  • Justice TheMainCharacter
  • Justice LordDeadlyOwl
Held The candidacy of u/melloyellobiblu was legal.
Ruling 3-0
Applicable precedent
  • Should a candidate for elected office circumvent the statutory restrictions, they shall not be included on the ballot.
  • If an alien wins an election, unless allowed by law, they shall be removed and the results recalculated.
  • Alternate accounts of a person are an extension of said person.

MAJORITY OPINION by Justice TheMainCharacter

(with Chief Justice SeaOtter and Justice LordDeadlyOwl agreeing)

Summary of the arguments

[1] The petitioner filed for judicial review of the legality of /u/melloyellobiblu's candidacy in the 2nd Parliamentary Election, on grounds of violating Art 2 §2 of the Citizenship Act.

[2] However, the petitioner contends that since /u/melloyellobiblu was elected following the full democratic process, they should be allowed to take their place as a Member of Parliament.

[3] The respondent states that because /u/melloyellobiblu was registered as a citizen under an alternate account their candidacy was valid. The Electoral Commission has presented evidence that /u/BasedGodWiggins is registered as a citizen and an alternate account of /u/melloyellobiblu.

Restriction on candidates for elected office.

[4] Article 2 §2 of the Citizenship Act restricts non-citizens from running for elected office. Similarly other statutes impose restrictions on the validity of candidates such as the New Party Act stating that one may not run as a representative of a Community Party.

[5] The court interprets this to mean that should anyone put forward a candidacy statement in contravention of these restrictions, their candidacy is invalid. This imposes a duty on the Electoral Commission to only include valid candidates on the ballot.

[6] Therefore, should an alien be accidentally included on the ballot and win, they cannot hold office since their very inclusion on the ballot was unlawful. Should this happen, election results shall have to be recalculated following the removal of the alien from the ballot.

[7] The court finds that in the eyes of the law, alternate accounts are extensions of the same person; a view affirmed by Article 1 §4.1. of the Criminal Code. Hence a person who receives citizenship by meeting the requirements on one of their accounts subsequently has the privileges afforded to citizens extended to all their accounts.

Verdict

[8] The court finds that /u/melloyellobiblu was registered as a citizen on their alternate account, /u/BasedGodWiggins at the time of them putting forward their candidacy.

[9] Hence, the court rules that their appearing on the ballot and subsequent election to Parliament is lawful.
