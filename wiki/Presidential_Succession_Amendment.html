If you have just installed MediaWiki
You probably installed from git, or directly from the source code using some other method. This is expected. Try installing some skins from mediawiki.org's skin directory, by:
  • Downloading the tarball installer, which comes with several skins and extensions. You can copy and paste the skins/ directory from it.
  • Downloading individual skin tarballs from mediawiki.org.
  • Using Git to download skins.
Doing this should not interfere with your git repository if you're a MediaWiki developer.
If you have just upgraded MediaWiki
MediaWiki 1.24 and newer no longer automatically enables installed skins (see Manual: Skin autodiscovery). You can paste the following lines into LocalSettings.php to enable all installed skins:
wfLoadSkin( 'MinervaNeue' );
wfLoadSkin( 'MonoBook' );
wfLoadSkin( 'Timeless' );
wfLoadSkin( 'Vector' );

Double-check the skin names for typos.

Preamble

Whereas multiple Presidential Succession Clauses are present that are inconsistent with the Constitution of Simdemocracy;

Whereas §7 of Article 7 of the Constitution is outdated and in need of revitalization;

Hereby enacts the following into law:

Presidential Succession Amendment

Section 1:

§1. Repeals and strikes out Article 2: Presidential Succession of Part 1: The President, and Schedule 1: Presidential line of succession of Part 3: Schedules of the Executive Act

§2. Repeals and strikes out Article 2: Presidential Succession of Part 1: The President, and Schedule 1: Presidential line of succession of Part 3: Schedules of the Statute Revision Act


Section 2:

§1. Amends from §7. to §7.14. of Article 7: Presidential Succession and Incapacity in the Constitution to read the following:

“§7. In the event of temporary or permanent incapacity, the following line of succession shall determine who assumes the duties or office of the President;

§7.1. Vice President,
§7.2. Speaker of the Senate,
§7.3. Executive Officers in order of seniority,
§7.4. Senators, in order of placement in the last Senate election,
§7.5 Supreme Court Justices, in order of seniority,
§7.6. Judges, in order of seniority,
§7.7. Discord Supervisor,
§7.8. Reddit Supervisor.”
