Applicable persuasive precedent
  • Prior court proceedings may be cited without being entered as evidence, as long as they do not establish new facts, [2]
    • The concept of Judicial Notice was codified by Article 13§5 in the Courtroom Procedures Act 2025
    • See also SD v fortnitewfortnitew [2025] Crim 117
  • Lack of Benefit to actions is a contributing factor towards there being proof they didn’t happen, [4]
  • A confession is not by itself proof of a crime having occurred, [5]
    • See SD v Seoulpig [2021] Crim 1

JUDGMENT by Justice TheMainCharacter

Use of past court proceedings as evidence

[1] The prosecution cited prior court proceedings, namely the evidence used in SD v Dick_head68 [2020] Crim 3, without entering it as evidence during the pre-trial.

[2] Since it is not used to establish new facts, and only bring to the attention of the court facts already established, the court allows this.

Review of the evidence

[3] The prosecution presents evidence of I_wanna_kill_myself.com admitting to having raided WeeklyDictator in order to cause a meme war. The accused also admits to being Dick_head.

[4] The defense questions whether the accused is actually dick_head. The court finds that no reasonable doubt has been raised about the identity of the accused, as their behaviour of declaring themselves to be dick_head is consistent with their past use of alts, and that impersonating dick_head would have no benefit.

[5] The evidence shows Dick_head admitting to having raided but does not show Dick_head actually raiding. A confession to a crime cannot by itself be taken as proof of the crime, unless the action of claiming itself is criminalised.

[6] Furthermore, the prosecution has failed to show that the community that was raided reached out to SimDemocracy's officials, which is required by statute (Article 72 of the Criminal Code) for the accused to be convicted of the crime of Brigading and Raiding.

Verdict

[7] The court finds the accused not guilty of Brigading and Raiding.
