MediaWiki 1.24 and newer no longer automatically enables installed skins (see Manual: Skin autodiscovery). You can paste the following lines into LocalSettings.php to enable all installed skins:
wfLoadSkin( 'MinervaNeue' );
wfLoadSkin( 'MonoBook' );
wfLoadSkin( 'Timeless' );
wfLoadSkin( 'Vector' );

EO 139-14 Public Weekly Reports

This matter has been repealed, voided, or is otherwise out of date

Repealed by EO 145-15 on 9 July 2025.

Issued by pat2pat on 14 April 2025

Whereas the President would like for the administration to be as open and honest as possible,

§1. EO 139-06 is repealed in its entirety.

§2. Each week the Chief of Staff will create two weekly reports for the President called the “Public Report” and the “Private Report”.

§3. Both reports will contain the exact same information as the other with the only exception being that the public report will not contain classified information from or relating to the Department of Justice or the Department of Defence.

§4. Each week the Public Report will be released for the public to view and to see what the administration is working on.

§5. This report will be released on ⁠⁠# presidental-announcements.

§6. This Executive Order shall be interpreted to apply to the past Reports created by the Chief of Staff under EO 139-06.

§7. The OHS shall be included in a separate section on the private CoS report, any information deemed to be safe enough for release by the President, shall be included on the public report.
