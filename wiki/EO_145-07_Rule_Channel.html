EO 145-07 Rule Channel

Issued by burdish(birdish152)

Whereas this would be useful for new members to be aware of common crimes without having to read the entire criminal code;

§1. A channel shall be created called # rules which shall be viewable to all members of the server.

§1.1. This channel shall be placed in the infos category, above ⁠official-announcements.

§2. The channel shall contain a message which states the following:

SimDemocracy, being a nation, has laws and penalties for violating those laws. Our entire Criminal Code can be found here.

However, here are some notable crimes to refrain from violating:

1. Violating the Discord Community Guidelines is a crime.
2. Hate speech is a crime. This includes the use of slurs or bigotry of any kind.
3. Posting obscene materials is a crime. This includes content which consists of gore, violence, or other materials not suitable for viewing in a public place, and would cause distress to a reasonable person.
4. Threatening people is a crime.
5. Harassment is a crime.
6. Doxxing or violation of someone’s personal privacy or information is a crime.
7. The use of alt accounts to gain any unfair advantage or undermine democracy in any way is a crime.
8. Spamming is a crime.
9. Brigading and raiding is a crime.

Be sure to check the criminal code for all the other crimes! Please remember that any member who commits a crime listed above, or in the criminal code, is prosecutable in court. The punishments for committing crimes vary.

If you see someone commit a crime, be sure to report it in # ⁠the-station.
