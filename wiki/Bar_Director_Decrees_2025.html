Decree 13B (Administrative)

Subject: Modification of the process of admission to the Bar Association and the delegation of authority to the Office of Legal Training.

Whereas Bar Director Decree No. 13 dated 13.05.2025 has been issued previously on the aforesaid subject;

And whereas the Director of the SimDemocracy Bar Association (hereinafter referred to as “the Director”) is empowered under Article 3, Section 1 of the Constitution of the SimDemocracy Bar Association, read with Section 2, Clause 2 of the SDBA By-Laws, and in accordance with the opinion of the Ethics Committee of the SDBA dated 15.05.2025 authored by cpalmer022:

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred and for the purposes of streamlining the process of admission to the Bar and regulating legal training, the following provisions are hereby notified:

1. The Bar Examination shall comprise two components, namely—

a. a written component; and

b. a practical component.

2. The written component shall be in the form of a Google Form which is publicly available. Specific instructions and information regarding the written component shall be contained inside the Google form. Such information and instructions are subject to change without prior notice. A candidate must score 50% in the written component to clear it. Upon clearing the written component, the candidate shall be granted the status of Provisional Attorney.

a. A candidate may attempt the Google Form no more than once every twenty-four (24) hours, Candidates may not use unfair means[1] in the written component. Any candidate found to have used unfair means may have their status of Provisional Attorney stripped. If a Certified Attorney is found to have used unfair means, then they maybe disbarred by the Ethics Committee.

b. For the avoidance of doubt, it is hereby declared that the status of Provisional Attorney shall not, in itself, constitute authorisation to practise law in SimDemocracy. No Provisional Attorney shall be deemed competent to practise law except under the supervision of a Certified Attorney.

3. The practical component shall be administered by the Office of Legal Training (subject to oversight by the Director). Only Provisional Attorneys may attempt the practical component. The practical component shall include a mandatory incubation period for all Provisional Attorneys. The length of the incubation period shall be notified by the Assistant Director, Office of Legal Training, SDBA (herein referred to as “Assistant Director”). After a Provisional Attorney completes the mandatory incubation period, the Assistant Director, with the express approval of the Director, shall have the authority to:

a. Grant the status of Certified Attorney and formal certification to practice law, or

b. Mandate that the candidate undergo an additional period of incubation, or

c. Strip the candidate of their status as Provisional Attorney.

Issued by the undersigned in exercise of powers vested in the office of the Bar Director,

MrGamerBoy, Director, SimDemocracy Bar Association.


' Rules and regulations concerning the definition of “unfair means” shall be notified within the written component of the Bar Exam. The definition may also be derived from legislation adopted by the SDBA and the Senate of SimDemocracy.
