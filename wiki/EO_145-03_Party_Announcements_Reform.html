EO 145-03 Party Announcements Reform


Issued by burdish(birdish152)

Repealed<ref name="eo13208">EO 136-08 Party Announcements Reformation</ref> EO 136 - 08 Party Announcements Reformation <ref name="eo13208">EO 136-08 Party Announcements Reformation</ref> EO 136-10 Party Representation Within SimDemocracy.<ref name="eo13210"> EO 136-10 Party Representation Within SimDemocracy</ref>

Whereas party announcements is rarely used,

Whereas party announcements was more active when the 'subscribe' feature was used,

Whereas we need a minimum member count to avoid hitting the limit on servers that one channel can subscribe to,

§1. Executive Order 136-08 and Executive Order 136-10 are hereby repealed in their entirety..<ref name="eo13210" />

§2. The channel currently titled “party-announcements” shall continue to exist under this Executive Order.

§3. If a party has 8 members or more (which shall be measured using the number of people that have the party role on discord), the Party Leader may ask the Discord Supervisor to subscribe to party-announcements from the announcements channel in the party server.

§3.1. The Discord Supervisor may remove these subscriptions should the messages announced be in violation of SimDemocracy law or Terms of Service.

§4. Nobody shall be able to speak in party-announcements without using the discord subscribe feature

Executive Orders Repealed
