Repealed by the Institutional Minimization and Downsizing Of Needless Excess (IM DONE) Act.
Repeal passed by the Senate on 28 May 2025, and signed by the President on 29 May 2025.

Oath for Office Act

Article 1: Clarification

§1.1 Any person holding any of the offices specified in §2.1 at the time of the passage of this act shall be required to swear an oath.

§1.2 All instances mentioning “affirm/swear” require either of those words to be used and if both are used then they must be separated using “and”.

Article 2: Jurisdiction

§2.1 Any person who is either elected or appointed to be any of the following:

Discord Supervisor,
Reddit Supervisor,
Election Supervisor,
President,
Vice President,
Member of Cabinet,
Judge,
Justice,
Senator,
Governor of the Minecraft Server,


Shall take an oath according to their respective office as specified in Articles 3 and 4.


Article 3: Administration of the Oath

§3.1 The oath for the Discord, Election and Reddit Supervisors shall be administered in Official Announcements channel by the Chief Justice.

§3.2 The oath for the President and Vice President shall be administered in Official Announcements channel by the Election Commissioner.

§3.3 The oath for Cabinet Members shall be administered in Government Announcements by the President.

§3.4 The oaths for Judges and Justices shall be administered by the Chief Justice in the Supreme Court.

§3.5 The oath for Senators and the Speaker shall be administered in Senate formal by the Vice President.

§3.6 The oath for the Governor of the Minecraft Server shall be administered by the Owner of the Minecraft server on the official Minecraft Server.

§3.7 The administrator shall administer the oath by asking the oath taker to recite the oath and after the recitation of the oath the administrator shall proclaim them to the office they were appointed or elected to.

§3.8 The Oath may be administered and recorded in any Voice Channel if both the administrator and oath taker agree.

Article 4: Oath of Office

§4.1 The Supervisors shall take the following oath :

“I solemnly swear/affirm that I will faithfully execute the duties of this office to the extent that I am available in SimDemocracy. If I do not have the availability necessary to execute my duties, I will resign from my position. I will uphold the constitution and the laws of SimDemocracy in all circumstances. I will always act in the best interest of SimDemocracy and its citizens. I will never use this office for personal gain nor will I let my biases and personal opinion cloud my judgement. I shall never use the powers granted to me unless authorized and requested by the Law or Relevant Legal Authorities. In light of the words “When Tyranny becomes Law, Rebellion becomes Duty”, I will never be a Tyrant, will never endorse Tyranny and will use all means at my disposal to combat Tyranny. This oath shall not apply to circumstances in which following it would threaten SimDemocracy’s existence or be immoral. I agree to be bound by this oath until such time as I vacate my position, at which point I shall be released from it. Ave SimDemocracy!”
