SammySnail v NovaSM [2019] Civ 2

Date of judgment 15th July 2019
Judge Judge Euphyrric
Grounds Civil harassment (common law)
Verdict D held liable for civil harassment
Result
  • D was detained in the “naughty corner” for one (1) hour<ref>Detention and giving punishment roles are now no longer permitted in civil cases. This case was done at a time when there was no separation of criminal and civil law.</ref>
  • D was given the “evil sinner” role for twenty-four (24) hours
Applicable persuasive precedent
  • Spam pinging constitutes civil harassment, [3]

JUDGMENT by Judge Euphyrric

[1] The court finds in favour of SammySnail#6667 and sentences NovaSM#8417 to 1 hour in the naughty corner, as well as having to wear the evil sinner role for 24 hours

[2] The court would like to note that in future, NovaSM#8417 may wish to refrain from doing unto others, that which they would not like done unto them

[3] The court would like to set the precedent that Spam-Pinging is the action of pinging someone repeatedly after they have been asked to stop. This action is to be outlawed and placed as a type of Harassment as defined by @/u/lolcheapboosts in Nova Vs. Dick_Head
