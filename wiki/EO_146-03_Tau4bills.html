EO 146-03 Tau4bills

From SimDemocracy Archives
Jump to navigation Jump to search

This matter has been repealed, voided, or is otherwise out of date

Repealed by EO 148-07 on 13 August 2025.

Issued by thymaerys on 13 July 2025

Whereas the people should be incentivized to engage in the legislative process,

Whereas we need some fresh ideas and some fresh faces doing shit.

§1. A new forum channel shall be created in the General category and placed just above the ⁠presidential-questions channel and shall be named, "tau4bills".

§2. Only the President, Vice President, and CoS may make forum posts in this channel.

§3. Everyone shall be able to comment on forum posts.

§4. The Department of Expansion is directed to utilize all available means to advertise the tau4bills initiative.

§5. The Treasury is directed to pay the author of a successful bill (defined as a bill which passes in the Senate after being approved by the President) a one-time prize of 150 tau. Individuals may only receive this prize one time per Presidential term.

§6. The Chief of Staff is directed to create and maintain a spreadsheet containing the names of all individuals who have received prize money for a successful bill under this program. Included on each line shall be the Discord username of the individual, the date of the bill's passage in the Senate, and the number associated with the Presidential term (for example, 146, 147, etc.).
