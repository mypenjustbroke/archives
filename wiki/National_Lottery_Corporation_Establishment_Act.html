If you have just installed MediaWiki
You probably installed from git, or directly from the source code using some other method. This is expected. Try installing some skins from mediawiki.org's skin directory, by:
  • Downloading the tarball installer, which comes with several skins and extensions. You can copy and paste the skins/ directory from it.
  • Downloading individual skin tarballs from mediawiki.org.
  • Using Git to download skins.
Doing this should not interfere with your git repository if you're a MediaWiki developer.
If you have just upgraded MediaWiki
MediaWiki 1.24 and newer no longer automatically enables installed skins (see Manual: Skin autodiscovery). You can paste the following lines into LocalSettings.php to enable all installed skins:
wfLoadSkin( 'MinervaNeue' );
wfLoadSkin( 'MonoBook' );
wfLoadSkin( 'Timeless' );
wfLoadSkin( 'Vector' );

Double-check the skin names for typos.

National Lottery Corporation Establishment Act

National Lottery Corporation Establishment Act

Passed by the Senate and signed by the President on 25 May 2025

Article 1: Formation of the SimDemocracy Lottery Corporation

§1. The SimDemocracy Lottery Corporation shall be a for profit corporation under SimDemocracy law that at the time of outset shall be owned solely by the State of SimDemocracy.

§2. The aim of the SimDemocracy Lottery Corporation shall be to hold regular lotteries, provide entertainment and to promote the circulation of currency,

§3. The SimDemocracy Lottery Corporation shall be managed by an officer of the Department of Treasury.

§4. The SimDemocracy Lottery Corporation shall be considered to hold a perpetual Casino License and its managing officer shall be considered its operator.

§5. The Secretary of the Treasury may choose to sell shares of the SimDemocracy Lottery Corporation; provided that the majority of the ownership is held by the State of SimDemocracy.

§5.1. Sales shall be done via public auction to the highest bidder.
§5.2. Shares shall be sold in blocs such that no more than 5% of the shares shall be sold in one sale.
