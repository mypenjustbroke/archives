  • Using Git to download skins.
Doing this should not interfere with your git repository if you're a MediaWiki developer.
If you have just upgraded MediaWiki
MediaWiki 1.24 and newer no longer automatically enables installed skins (see Manual: Skin autodiscovery). You can paste the following lines into LocalSettings.php to enable all installed skins:
wfLoadSkin( 'MinervaNeue' );
wfLoadSkin( 'MonoBook' );
wfLoadSkin( 'Timeless' );
wfLoadSkin( 'Vector' );

SD v NovaSM 2019 Crim 3

SD v NovaSM [2019] Crim 3

Date of judgment 7th August 2019
Judge Judge lolcheapboosts
Charges 1 charge of Spam (Article I.B.8 of the Community Standards Amendment)
Verdict Guilty of 1 charge of Spam
Sentence 12 hours of isolation
Applicable persuasive precedent
  • It does not matter whether D commits a crime in jest or not, [3]
    • Partially overturned by SD v Mr_Cynical6 [2019] Crim 6

JUDGMENT by Judge lolcheapboosts<ref>Paragraph numbering added retrospectively.</ref>

[1] On one count of spam, the defendant is found guilty and is to be sentenced to 12hr isolation

[2] Although the defendant had stopped after being asked, the initial spam had a negative effect on the community and on the community members

[3] Breaking the law as a joke is still breaking the law
