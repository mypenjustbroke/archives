MediaWiki 1.24 and newer no longer automatically enables installed skins (see Manual: Skin autodiscovery). You can paste the following lines into LocalSettings.php to enable all installed skins:
wfLoadSkin( 'MinervaNeue' );
wfLoadSkin( 'MonoBook' );
wfLoadSkin( 'Timeless' );
wfLoadSkin( 'Vector' );

EO 160-10 The Arbor Council

Issued by thymaerys on 30 January 2026

Whereas trees are cool;

§1. An unpaid unroled Arbor Council shall be established to discuss trees and related flora (ferns, moss, bushes, etc).

§2. Members of the Arbor Council shall be appointed by the President.

§3. The members of the Arbor Council shall elect amongst themselves a Lorax who shall speak for both the Council and the trees.
