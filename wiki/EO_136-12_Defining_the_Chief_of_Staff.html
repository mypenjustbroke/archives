EO 136-12 Defining the Chief of Staff

From SimDemocracy Archives
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Reinstated by EO 151-23 on 4 October 2025.

Repealed by EO 151-22 on 4 October 2025.

Reinstated by EO 150-05 on 15 September 2025.

Repealed by EO 149-06 on 28 August 2025.

Issued by Muggy(._.symph0ny._.) on 1 March 2025

Whereas the Chief of Staff holds no real authority and similarly, not much of a purpose.

Whereas the Chief of Staff is not much of an asset to the President or Executive Branch.

§1. A new role titled “Chief Of Staff” will be created right below the “Vice President” role.

§2. The Chief of Staff role will be given permissions to see channels necessary to its work, including all Executive Department channels to report on their affairs.

Repealed by EO 142-01 on 19 May 2025.

§3. The Chief of Staff will be confirmed by the Senate of SimDemocracy, considered a cabinet-level position.

Repealed by EO 151-16 on 4 October 2025.

§3. The Chief of Staff shall be a non-executive officer cabinet position appointed by the President. <ref>Amended by EO 142-01</ref>

§3. The Chief of Staff will be confirmed by the Senate of SimDemocracy, considered a cabinet-level position. <ref>Amended by EO 151-16</ref>

§4. The Chief of Staff may give directives to cabinet members, but directives given by the President shall always hold precedent.

§5. In cabinet affairs, the Vice President may also give directives to the cabinet that hold precedent over those given by the Chief of Staff.

§6. Cabinet members may refuse to follow directives given by the Chief of Staff if they do not have the resources to properly execute such a directive, believe it is a wrongful directive, or believe that their current work is impaired by said directive.

§7.The Chief of Staff may not make decisions in the place of Executive Departments unless expressly permitted by the President or the Head of said Executive Department.

Repealed by tywearingatie.

§8. The Chief of Staff is to send a written report to the President on the affairs of each Executive Department twice each Presidential term.

§8. The Chief of Staff, if requested by the President, shall send them a written report on the affairs of each Executive Department twice each Presidential term. <ref name="ty"> Amended by tywearingatie</ref>

§9. The report written by the Chief of Staff will consist of:

§9.1 A day-to-day operations summary of Executive Departments.
§9.2 Inefficiencies needing to be addressed.
§9.3 Employees that are inactive or otherwise unnecessary.
§9.4 Any other details the Chief of Staff deems necessary to include in said report.

§10. Section 2 of EO 133-01 is repealed.

§11. Multiple Chief of Staffs may be appointed.<ref name="ty"></ref>
