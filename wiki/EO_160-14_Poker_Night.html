EO 160-14 Poker Night


Issued by thymaerys on 4 February 2026

Whereas it was rad as fuck when Stalin did it;

§1. The Department of Expansion (DoEx) shall host a poker event consisting of six (6) to seven (7) poker games conducted over the course of three (3) days. The games shall be held on a website chosen by the Secretary of Expansion, where participants may play poker at a table with other members of SimDemocracy.

§2. Each game shall take place in voice chat, and the table shall be streamed by the Host. Each participant shall begin the event with one thousand (1,000) chips. Participants may purchase additional chips at a rate of one hundred (100) chips per fifty (50) tau, up to a maximum of three thousand (3,000) additional chips per participant.

§3. The winner of the event shall be the participant with the highest net number of chips earned at the conclusion of the final game. Net chips shall be calculated by subtracting the participant’s starting chips and any purchased chips from their final chip total. The winner shall recieve 1,000 tau. The participant who places 2nd shall receive 500 tau.

§4. Upon the conclusion of each game, the Host shall update the chip balance for each participant.

§5. Any questions, disputes, or unforeseen issues arising during the event shall be resolved by a ruling issued by the Host, whose decision shall be final.
