DoT Resolution 01-26

TO: All DoT Personnel

FROM: Office of the Secretary

DATE: 23rd of February, 2026

SUBJECT: DoT Current Organization Chart

To establish a more defined and rigid system of organization within the Department of the Treasury, and to reflect the current state and realities of its current operations, the Department hereby issues the following resolution

I. Organization Chart

§1. Current Organization Chart:

File:DO-DoT-01-26.png

II. Office of the Secretary

§1. The Secretary of the Treasury shall serve as the head of the Department and retains ultimate responsibility for:

§1.1 Maintenance of the economic holdings of the state;
§1.2 Oversight of taxation and revenue programs;
§1.3 Maintenance of payroll operations;
§1.4 Regulation of corporations and markets;
§1.5 Maintenance of the public financial ledger;
§1.6 Strategic policy direction of the Department.

§2. The Secretary may issue further orders or directives consistent with law..

III. Chief of Staff

§1. The Chief of Staff shall:

§1.1 Coordinate internal operations of the Office of the Secretary;
§1.2 Manage administrative workflow and inter-departmental coordination;
§1.3 Support execution of policy priorities established by the Secretary.

§2. The Chief of Staff exercises no independent statutory authority unless delegated.

IV. General Counsel

§1. The General Counsel shall:

§1.1 Provide legal advice to the Department;
§1.2 Ensure regulatory and operational compliance with statute;
§1.3 Coordinate with the Department of Justice where required under Article 6 §5 of the Executive Departments Act 2025.

V. Deputy Secretary of the Treasury (Operations)

§1. The Deputy Secretary shall serve as the principal deputy to the Secretary and acting head of the Department in the absence of the Secretary.

§2. The Deputy Secretary (Operations) shall oversee fiscal operations including:

§2.1 Treasurer’s Office;
§2.2 Central Revenue Bureau;
§2.3 Division of Business;
§2.4 Other revenue-in and revenue-out functions assigned by the Secretary.

§3. Responsibilities include:

§3.1 Operational execution of payroll;
§3.2 Administration of tax collection;
§3.3 Management of state financial flows;
§3.4 Ensuring compliance with statutory deadlines.

VI. Assistant Secretary of the Treasury (Financial Systems and Markets)

§1. The Assistant Secretary shall oversee financial regulation, monitoring, and economic reporting functions.

§2. Areas of responsibility include:

§2.1 Financial Overseer of the SimDem Stock Exchange;
§2.2 Regulatory committees established by the Department;
§2.3 Monitoring of market activity and financial systems;
§2.4 Economic reporting and analysis functions;
§2.5 Coordination with financial regulatory bodies.

§3. The Assistant Secretary acts under delegated authority from the Secretary.

VII. Re: SimDem State Bank Relationship

§1. The SimDem State Bank is an independent body established under the SimDem State Bank Act 2025.

§2. Nothing within this Order shall be interpreted as directing, controlling, or interfering with the independent exercise of the Bank’s statutory powers.

§3. The Department shall maintain a liaison relationship for coordination purposes only, including:

§3.1 Information sharing;
§3.2 Economic coordination;
§3.3 Administrative support where appropriate.

VIII. Reporting Structure

§1. All Department divisions shall report through the organizational structure outlined above unless otherwise directed by statute or subsequent Departmental Order.

IX. Line of Succession

§1. The line of Succession in the event that the Secretary’s position is vacant, or otherwise unable to be executed, is as follows;

§1.1 Deputy Secretary of the Treasury (Operations)
§1.2 Treasurer of SimDemocracy
§1.3 Assistant Secretary of the Treasury for Financial Systems and Markets
§1.4 General Counsel
§1.5 Chief of Staff to the Secretary of the Treasury
§1.6 Director of the Central Revenue Bureau
§1.7 Director of the Division of Business
§1.8 Financial Overseer of the SimDem Stock Exchange
§1.9 Deputy Directors of Divisions (CRB, Treasurer Office, DoB, ect) by Seniority

X. Effective Date

§1. This Order shall take effect immediately upon issuance.

Signed:

Lyoko222/CtheEng

162nd Secretary of the Treasury
