  • Using Git to download skins.
Doing this should not interfere with your git repository if you're a MediaWiki developer.
If you have just upgraded MediaWiki
MediaWiki 1.24 and newer no longer automatically enables installed skins (see Manual: Skin autodiscovery). You can paste the following lines into LocalSettings.php to enable all installed skins:
wfLoadSkin( 'MinervaNeue' );
wfLoadSkin( 'MonoBook' );
wfLoadSkin( 'Timeless' );
wfLoadSkin( 'Vector' );

Double-check the skin names for typos.

Quads Role Order Act


Quad's Role Order Act

Authored by Quad

Passed by the Senate and signed on September the 12th, 2025

Preamble

Whereas, a certain someone has complained;

Explanatory Notes: This Act introduces formal rules for the ordering of roles in the discord role list. Privately owned roles would be put above most common roles so their colour could be seen.

Provisions

§1. The §§ 10 and 10.1 of Article 3 (Part 1) of the Sixth Discord Act shall be amended to read as follows:

§10. The roles within the SimDemocracy Discord server shall be placed in an order according to Section two (2) of the Appendix.

§10.1. When it is unclear where in the order a role should fall, the President may decide.


§2. There shall be a new §2 inserted into the Appendix of the Sixth Discord Act. It shall read as follows:

§2. The order of the roles within the Discord role list shall be:

§2.1. All other roles not listed here
§2.2. All privately owned roles that aren’t party roles
§2.3. Party roles
§2.4. Roles indicating voter registration or verification status
§2.5. Pronoun roles
§2.6. Ping roles
