MediaWiki 1.24 and newer no longer automatically enables installed skins (see Manual: Skin autodiscovery). You can paste the following lines into LocalSettings.php to enable all installed skins:
wfLoadSkin( 'MinervaNeue' );
wfLoadSkin( 'MonoBook' );
wfLoadSkin( 'Timeless' );
wfLoadSkin( 'Vector' );

Treasury Liaison (Amendment) Act

Amendment to the Treasury Liaison Act

Preamble:

WHEREAS the Treasury Liaison system was created to ensure accurate and timely compensation of government employees; and

WHEREAS greater transparency and accountability in liaison operations will foster trust and efficiency in public administration;

The following Articles shall be added to the Act:

Article 3: Standardized Reporting Procedures

§1. All Treasury Liaisons must submit a weekly report to the Secretary of the Treasury. This report shall include:

§1.1. A list of all currently active employees in the assigned department.
§1.2. The weekly compensation due to each employee.
§1.3. Any discrepancies or delays in payments, with explanations.
§1.4. Notes on changes in employment status (hiring, resignation, termination).

§2. Reports must follow a standardized template issued by the Department of Treasury, which includes:

§2.1. Employee ID (Discord username)
§2.2. Role/Title
§2.3. Wage bracket
§2.4. Payment status

§3. Reports are due every Saturday by 19:59 UTC. Failure to submit two consecutive reports without valid justification will result in review and potential reassignment.
